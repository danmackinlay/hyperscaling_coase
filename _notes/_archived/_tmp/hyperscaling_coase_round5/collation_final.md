# Sampling round, 2026-10-23: collation by entailment

Twelve runs (deal in `deal.json`, seed 1), all returned, none a sequel. Clustered by entailment; score = runs × distinct pools. Replication by the session in `collator_rerun.py` / `collator_rerun_out.txt`; adversarial second readers on the top four (verdicts appended below when in).

## E1. The company would have to need every neighbour's yes, or the neighbours be bound all-or-none before the offers arrive

Runs 01 (Winter; pool A, opus), 02 (Genicot–Ray; A, fable), 03 (Chipty–Snyder; A, opus), 04 (Segal 2003; A, fable), 11 (formation arm; D, opus); run 08's majority-of-agents row (B) reaches it too. **Score 5 × 3 = 15.** Mechanisms within it: clauses conditioned on who else signs, so signing is dominant and nobody is paid (01: (−2,−3,−8), company 18.5); door-to-door sequencing, the pivot bought in full and the rest at close to their fallback, Carol never approached (02: speed, Alice and Bob squeezed as δ → 1); flat fees to the cheapest pair at their harms, Carol at −8, with the pass rate and Carol's loss rising with the company's estimation budget (04: −1.7 → −7.6); random-proposer bargaining at k=2 leaving Carol −4.9 to −7.4 (03); a pact or union with a common floor that raises Alice's and Bob's price and leaves Carol out because she is dearest to include (11). Every run: Carol at −8 against the ordinance's −4, below no deal; the night slot runs because her harm is external to the deal. The repair that works in every run is a club that recreates unanimity by contract (all-or-none terms; a binding mandate before the offers; near-certain binding, r > 0.92 in 01) or the council setting k = n; a club with a common floor but no all-or-none clause does not (11), and a club at one common price lifts Carol only to −5 (04). Chipty–Snyder's curvature sign (03): the club helps the residents at k=2 and costs them 1.5 at k=3, so "always form a club" is not a default. Session replication: the k=2 vectors reproduce by hand (fees (2,3,0) on MDEN: company 13.5, residents (0,0,−8); contingent clauses: 18.5 and (−2,−3,−8); club at 3 a head: 9.5 and (1,0,−5)). The sequencing thresholds (02: δ > 0.884) and the SPE values (03) are the runs' own, pending the reader. Run 11's n=30 result (exclusion a one-in-30 lottery, pacts divided, per-resident message cost falling with n) means null 2's refutation condition as written is not met at k = n−1; the cost does not grow with n, the exclusion does not go away. Status quo: every k=2 outcome leaves Carol below the ordinance; Alice and Bob at or above it under flat offers, below it under contingent clauses or a fast sequencer. **Already on the state's lists:** yes (divide and conquer, rows 10, 15, 21); **what is new:** the formation stage computed, the contingent-clause mechanism, the estimation ramp, the curvature sign for clubs, and the all-or-none condition on the repair.

## E2. Carol's street would have to be the only street the company can use

Runs 12 (two streets; D, fable), 07 (chain store; B, opus), 05 (the town with a detour; B, opus). **Score 3 × 2 = 6.** Mechanisms: the company runs a procurement auction among streets, offering the floor to all but one; a street's commitment protects its share only while every other street's holds, residents' take 4.5(1−(1−q)^3)^(K−1), zero at every K for q < 0.37 (12, closed form, session-replicated); a public record across M streets turns any prior q > 0 that a final offer binds into a reputation, per-street residents' take → 0 as M grows (07, Kreps–Wilson, q=0.01: 1.49 → 0.17 at M = 1 → 1000; floor never below 0); a detour below the highest possible harm kills the one-price club at scale and leaves only house-by-house pricing, which the company's estimation budget takes (05, threshold closed form at the maximum harm). On the base instance E2 is the outside-option switch (reroute 6.5 > pie 6, session-replicated). Nobody below no deal or below the ordinance in any E2 run: the loss is the share, invisible to the floor and the status-quo comparison. Repairs: a club spanning every street the company can reach (pattern bargaining), which must exist before the first offer (the pinned sentence); hiding the record (07, which crosses null 3: public information lowers the floor); refusing parallel negotiations (12). **Already on the lists:** the open system was an open row; **new:** three computed mechanisms with closed forms, and the reach K as a parameter the dials do not name.

## E3. The neighbours' agents would have to know the company's margin as well as it knows their harms, and face it in as many seats

Runs 10 (company's designer; C, fable), 09 (council; C, opus); run 05's estimation extraction under a fixed seat is adjacent (B). **Score 2 × 1 = 2 (3 × 2 = 6 with 05).** Under the proposer drawn by party with one-shot offers and unanimity, a resident proposer must shade her demand by her error about the company's profit and the company keeps that margin; the company's estimation of the harms collects its own seat in full; the company takes 2.04 at a 10:1 edge and 2.17 with exact knowledge against a seat share of 1.5, residents 1.04–1.21 each (10, 09 agree to 0.1). Repair: a disclosure duty on the company's books (09) caps it at 1.5 at every budget; one-sided disclosure reverses the asymmetry (10: residents 1.53, company 0.71), so symmetric verification is the condition. Nobody below no deal or the ordinance. **Against the state:** row 22 ("the seat extracts, not the budget") is narrowed: under the by-party protocol the budget moves the division past the seat share, which the hard case's own (0.1, 0.3) cell at 1.81 already showed and the state called "capped by seat shares"; the cap is the sample-fraction tilt's absence, not a cap at the seat share. **New:** the books as the entailment; the audit's cost curve (09: excess 0.67 → 0.08 as the audit error 0.316 → 0.02, cost ∝ 1/error²).

### E5 reader (opus, on run 08): demoted
Numbers reproduce in the unanimity rows (with equilibrium multiplicity: (3,4,4,4), (4,3,4,4), (4,4,4,4) at 1:1; (22,1,1,1) beside (21,1,1,1) at 10:1); the 1:10 row is not the mirror of 10:1 (the true mirror gives the company 0.045); the κ=0.03 and 0.1 sweep cells have no pure equilibrium (a two-cycle); the majority-of-agents row has a seat-count error (3 of 6 is a tie, 0.09 spent implies 4 agents), an unstated voting protocol, residents barred from counter-registering, and 18.5 gross of agent cost. Material defect: "equal gain per seated agent" is attributed to the agenda without a source; Krier's line is about equal voting weight, not division; and it contradicts the mission's "a split party counts once". Verdict: survives only under the platform-counts-agents reading with priced agents and a free first one; under per-person division nothing moves; the below-status-quo row is withdrawn. Carried as: a sampled finding, narrowed to "the platform's seating and division rule is a distributional choice; if agents are counted, the side with cheaper agents wins, and one-human-one-agent has no default reading for a firm", which extends row 26 and does not reach the third list.

### E3 reader (opus, on runs 10 and 09): narrowed
Each run reproduces itself (run10 to 0.01 at 50,000 draws; run09 to 0.02). They are not the same model: run10 puts the fleet's noise on harms rather than sensitivities and fixes the schedule at M, D, E, which its text denies; under the instance-literal model (run09's) the 10:1 cell at 0.3 against 1 gives the fleet 1.375, below its seat share, and 2.04 falls to 1.79 with noise on sensitivities and to 1.375 when the resident proposer also chooses the schedule. The fleet exceeds 1.5 only once its error is below about 0.2 against the residents' 0.316, or at 100:1 (1.81 against 0.85, the hard case's cell exactly). "One against three at equal compute" is an artefact of the information structure: it needs residents to know one another's harms exactly (R1) and the fleet's reservation to be the sum of three noisy slots (error ×√3); with every reservation equally noisy the fleet earns what each resident earns (0.868 against 0.866), and at the instance's 0.316 there is parity. The open-books cap at 1.5 is an identity (residents at error 0 give the fleet 0 on their seats and at most 6 on its own). Verdict: survives narrowed. Carried as: "while the residents' error about the company's margin is positive and the company's error on their sensitivities is below about 0.2, the company takes above its seat share and the residents fall equally short; opening the books holds it to the seat share by construction; the asymmetry is in whose number is private at the table, not in the seat count." Restates the hard case's row 22 with the books named; does not contradict it. Entailment reworded: the company's margin would have to be as known to the neighbours as their harms are to one another.

### E2 reader (fable, on runs 12 and 07): survives
Both models reproduce to the printed digit (run12's closed form and drawn-sensitivity shares by exact enumeration; run07's Kreps–Wilson chain store with every on-path incentive condition holding and the tail-street counts). Material corrections: run12's "fraction at or below no deal" was a fraction of all parties, not of residents (the residents-only figure, 0.50/0.67/0.80 at K = 2/3/5, is larger and strengthens the row); its "pie rises with K" is realised pie with walked deals at zero, mostly a deal-rate effect; its "expressible on base" overreaches, since the reroute switch is a sure outside option worth 6.5 that kills every deal while the K-street model is a stochastic one whose bite depends on q. Run07's structural additions to Kreps–Wilson are stated now: one premium posted once for all streets and both types (pooling by assumption), acquiescence pinned at the fair split by fiat, the M=1 row by direct check outside the theorem's assumption. The mapping to Kreps–Wilson (refuse = enter, walk = fight, raise = acquiesce, beliefs updated on walks only) is faithful. Verdict: survives, under the reading that offers are take-it-or-leave-it, residents cannot pool bargains across streets, and the premium or demand is fixed in advance; the fair deal at one street degrades monotonically with the company's reach whenever counterparties' commitment is imperfect or any positive prior on a binding final offer exists with a public record. Carried with the corrected denominator and the realised-pie caveat; the base-instance replication is the outside-option switch as a limiting case, not an expression of q.

### E1 reader (opus, on runs 04 and 01): entailment survives, the compute half does not
Rebuilt both from their text. Run01's contingent-clause outcome (18.47; −1.99, −2.99, −7.99), its pact thresholds (r* 0.757 at premium 0.5, 0.919 at 1.5) and its q-sweep reproduce; its "Carol −6 at q=0.75" needs perfectly correlated types (−4.49 if independent). Run04's k=2 vectors reproduce as limits (fees h+ε). Material defects: run04's "unique implementation by enumeration" is wrong, since at fees equal to harms there are five pure equilibria and, more important, the stated protocol also has the everyone-signs-for-tokens equilibrium paying the company 18.47 with (−2,−3,−8) and no contingent clauses at all; run04's 13.5 is the lowest-equilibrium selection (round-robin from no consent), and under the simultaneous dynamics it says it checked, token fees converge to everyone signing, so a principal optimising against that dynamic needs no estimate of anyone's harm and the estimation dial does nothing. Run04's ramp (Carol −1.7 → −7.6) needs a fixed absolute margin or a flat prior: with the margin scaled to the error Carol is roughly flat (−5.1/−5.7/−5.8 at one standard deviation); with an informative prior she is near −8 already at error 1 (−7.85). Run01 labels q, the probability a resident's agent best-replies rather than holds to the pact, as "compute", which is unstated, and its pact-holders sign the fair offer on their own rather than all-or-none. Neither run lets Carol counter-bid for Alice's or Bob's refusal. Verdict: at k=2 Carol at −8 with the night slot running holds under every reading checked; only a club at one price (−5) or an all-or-none pact binding above 0.92 (+1.5) moves her; the "compute sharpens it" claim needs the lowest-equilibrium selection plus a fixed margin or flat prior (run04), or q read as compute (run01); without those, Carol's −8 needs no compute at all. Carried as: a rule failure (the consent rule below unanimity, with the company moving first), with compute's role confined to which of Alice and Bob is squeezed (run02's speed) and to the pass rate under one selection; E1 leaves the third list and joins the first two as the sharpest statement of an entailment already there, with the all-or-none condition on the repair as the new content.

## Outcome of the round

Twelve runs, five entailments, four adversarially read, all four survive in some form and two narrowed hard. The third list, after reading: the conditions under which more compute for one side takes value from the other, below the fair deal, are the company's reach beyond one street (E2, intact, closed forms, never below the status quo), the company's private margin under a protocol that looks symmetric (E3, narrowed to the case where the company's error is small relative to the residents' error about its books, restating the hard case with the books named), the contract's scope under a speed gap (E4, one run, unread), and the platform's seating rule (E5, narrowed to "if agents are counted"). None of these takes anyone below the regulated status quo. The one entailment that does, consent below unanimity (E1), needs no compute to do it. So the round's finding for the essay is: the agenda fails against regulation only where the consent rule already fails, and against its own promise everywhere compute is unequal; and the honest third list is short and shares-only. Null 2's cost clause is refuted (cost falls with n) and its pact clause narrowed (only all-or-none, near-certain binding resists); null 1 is refuted in one shot only in the narrow window E3 names, with the learning arm pending the pilot; null 3 is touched twice (hiding the record restores the fair deal; symmetric disclosure restores it, one-sided reverses it) and not yet run.
